Iversen Lab – Undergraduate Research Opportunities

Explore the neuroscience of music, rhythm, movement, and perception

Why Join?

At the Iversen Lab we plan on using a variety of methods like EEG, MEG, tACS, and motion capture to investigate many different topics in rhythm, movement, perception, human development and more!

Current Projects

Metric Modulation – EEG analysis of how people switch between rhythmic structures.
Group Drum Circle – Studying inter-brain synchrony and social cooperation via drumming and neuromodulation (EEG+tACS).
Rhythm Skill Training – Developing a rhythm game for different populations (musicians, novices, hearing impaired).
Drum Set Performance & Pedagogy – Exploring brain/body coordination and creative motor skill during drumming.
Music & Relaxation – Ongoing analysis of EEG responses to music vs. silence.
Juggling & Reduced Gravity – Studying motor control and coordination in real and simulated gravity using EEG.
EEG/MEG Fusion – Developing methods to combine EEG, MEG, and MRI data for improved brain source localization.
Auditory Stream Analysis – Understanding how the brain groups sounds into auditory objects.
Auditory Attention – Investigating how we focus on certain sounds while ignoring others.
Music & Development – Analyzing large-scale child brain imaging data to understand the impact of music on development.
Human Navigation – A new project using motion capture and virtual audio to study spatial navigation and brain activity.

What We’re Looking For

  • Students interested in Psychology, Neuroscience, Music, and Computer Science
  • No prior lab experience required – just enthusiasm and curiosity!
  • Helpful skills: Programming (Python,R, or MATLAB), EEG/MEG analysis
